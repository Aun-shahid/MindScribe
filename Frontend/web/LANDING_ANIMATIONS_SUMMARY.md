## 🎯 Animation Principles Applied

### Performance Optimized
- ✅ Uses Framer Motion for smooth 60fps animations
- ✅ GPU-accelerated transforms (scale, rotate, translate)
- ✅ `viewport={{ once: true }}` to prevent re-triggering
- ✅ Staggered delays to avoid overwhelming users

### User Experience
- ✅ Animations enhance, not distract
- ✅ Purposeful motion guides attention
- ✅ Reduced motion respected automatically
- ✅ Interactive feedback on hover/tap
- ✅ Smooth scroll-triggered reveals

### Visual Hierarchy
- ✅ Important content animates first
- ✅ Subtle background animations
- ✅ Bold foreground interactions
- ✅ Color-coded feature categories
